🔁 Cycle Detection

Undirected (parent rule) and directed (3-colour DFS).

undirecteddirected

The idea

Not every revisit is a cycle. The rule differs by graph type — that’s the whole chapter.

Undirected: a visited neighbour that isn’t your parent = cycle. Directed: an edge to a vertex still on the recursion stack (a grey node) = cycle.

① Undirected — DFS remembering your parent

Walk with DFS. Ignore the neighbour you just came from; any other visited neighbour closes a cycle.

recursion stack

② Directed — the 3-colour DFS (back edges)

White = unseen, grey = on the stack, black = finished. An edge to a grey node is a back edge → cycle.
