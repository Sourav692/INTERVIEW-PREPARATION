198 · House Robber

Most loot, no two adjacent.

The problem, in plain words

Rob houses in a row for the most money, but you can’t rob two adjacent houses (alarms).

Example: [2,7,9,3,1] → 12 (rob 2 + 9 + 1).
looking atremembered / keptmatch / answerruled out

The smart way — at each house: skip, or rob + best from two back

Best loot up to a house = the larger of (skip it → best up to the previous house) or (rob it → its value + best up to two houses back).
house values
best loot up to each house

Speed: one pass — O(n), and only two numbers of memory.
