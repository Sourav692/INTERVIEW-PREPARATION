105 · Construct Binary Tree from Preorder and Inorder

Rebuild a tree from two lists.

The problem, in plain words

Rebuild the exact tree from two lists: preorder (root first) and inorder (left, root, right).

preorder [3,9,20,15,7] + inorder [9,3,15,20,7] → the original tree.
looking atremembered / keptmatch / answerruled out

The smart way — preorder gives roots, inorder splits left/right

The next preorder value is always a root. Find it in inorder: everything to its left is its left subtree, everything to its right is its right subtree. Repeat for each side.
preorder (roots in order)
inorder (splits left / right)

Speed: with a hash map for the inorder split, O(n) (naive index search is O(n²)).
