🛣️ Weighted Shortest Paths

Dijkstra (closest-first) and Bellman-Ford (relax all edges).

DijkstraBellman-Ford

The idea

On a weighted graph, "shortest" means cheapest total, not fewest edges. The shared core is relaxation: if going through u reaches v cheaper, update v.

Dijkstra settles the closest vertex next (non-negative weights). Bellman-Ford relaxes every edge V−1 times (handles negatives, detects negative cycles).

① Dijkstra — settle the closest vertex, relax its edges

Greedy + a min-heap. Once a vertex is settled its distance is final — which is why it needs non-negative weights.

best-known distance (∞ = unreached)

② Bellman-Ford — relax every edge, V−1 rounds

No cleverness about order: just relax all edges each round. Handles negative edges; a further improvement after V−1 rounds means a negative cycle.
