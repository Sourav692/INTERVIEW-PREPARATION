The idea

A Strongly Connected Component is a maximal group where every vertex reaches every other (directed). Collapse each SCC and the graph becomes a DAG.

Kosaraju: DFS finish-order → reverse edges → DFS again; each tree = one SCC. Tarjan: one DFS with discovery/low-link numbers.
