## Cross-cutting patterns

- **Hash maps** show up in nearly every problem (1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10, 11, 12) — almost always for O(1) tallying/grouping.
- **Heaps** appear 4 times (2, 3, 12, 13) — three genuine uses (top-K, interval partitioning) and one deliberately-flawed use (Content Popularity Tracker, to show *why* a heap doesn't fit).
- **Tree/graph traversal** appears 4 times (2, 5, 8, 9) — post-order bottom-up aggregation (2, 8), trie DFS (5), and full graph algorithms BFS + Tarjan's (9).
- **Sorting-based techniques** (sweep-line, multi-key sort, binary search over a monotonic sequence) appear in 4, 6, 7, 10 — several rely on *stable sort* as the enabling trick for multi-key ordering, rather than a custom comparator.
- Only problem 11 (SQL parts) steps outside plain Python data structures into SQL-native constructs (window functions, star schema).
