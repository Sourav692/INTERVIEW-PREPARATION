239 · Sliding Window Maximum

The biggest value in every sliding window.

The problem, in plain words

A window of width k slides across the numbers. Report the biggest value inside the window at every position.

DevRev framing: a live dashboard’s rolling peak — max concurrent tickets, max latency, or worst SLA breach over the last k samples.
looking atremembered / keptmatch / answerruled out

The smart way — a deque that keeps the biggest at the front

A deque is a line you can add to / remove from at both ends. We keep candidate positions in it, biggest at the front. When a bigger number arrives, smaller older ones can never win again — drop them from the back. Drop the front when it slides out of the window.

Speed: each position enters and leaves the deque once — O(n) (vs O(n·k) re-scanning every window).

When you’d use this

See "max/min over every sliding window" or a rolling peak? A monotonic deque.
